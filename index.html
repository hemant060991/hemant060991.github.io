HealthHub

HealthHub is a private, self-hosted app that one family uses to keep its own health data in one place: readings from their Fitbit devices (through the Google Health API) and their own lab reports.

It is not a public service. Only invited family members can sign in, and it runs on a home server reachable only on a private network.
