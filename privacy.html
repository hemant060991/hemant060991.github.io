HealthHub privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

What it reads

With your permission, HealthHub reads your activity, heart, sleep, body and nutrition data from the Google Health API, read-only. It also stores lab reports that you upload yourself.

Where it goes

  • Data is stored on a home server owned by the app's operator, reachable only on a private network. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.
  • Your data is visible to you and to anyone you choose to share it with inside the app. You can withdraw a share at any time.
  • When you ask the built-in assistant a question or upload a report to be read, the relevant data is sent to Anthropic's Claude to produce the answer.

Google user data

HealthHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to show you your own health data and charts. Humans do not read it except at your request or where needed for security or the law.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have everything HealthHub holds about you deleted, email hemant.aggarwal1991@gmail.com.
